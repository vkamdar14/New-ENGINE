## 1. "Why GTA 6 is 30fps" — 27.4s  ← the volume play

> everyone is mad gta six is thirty frames
> so here is what thirty actually buys you
> a console has one frame budget
> **sixteen milliseconds at sixty, thirty three at thirty**
> double the time means double the work per frame
> more draw distance more cars more npcs
> they did not fail to hit sixty
> **they spent sixty on the world instead**
> you can have the frames or the city
> on that hardware you cannot have both

Rides the biggest current wave without joining the argument — it *explains*
the argument, which is the position with the widest audience. Everyone in that
comment section is picking a side; this is the video that tells them what the
sides actually are.

The frame-budget maths is evergreen engineering, so it does not rot when the
discourse moves on.

## 2. "Samsung's 1100Hz monitor" — 32.3s

> samsung built an eleven hundred hertz monitor
> your eye does not see eleven hundred frames
> **that is not what it is for**
> at sixty hertz a frame sits for sixteen milliseconds
> at eleven hundred it sits for under one
> so the image stops smearing when you flick
> it is not more frames you notice, it is less blur
> the catch is you need eleven hundred fps to feed it
> no gpu does that at any real resolution
> **so you are buying clarity in old games, and a number for the box**

Freshest hook of the three (2 days old, 353k views/day) and the most
stranger-legible: "a screen so fast it stops blurring" needs no prior
knowledge. The turn at "that is not what it is for" is the whole video.

## 3. "It's not Apple slowing your phone" — 31.9s

> every new iphone feels slower after a year
> **and it is not apple slowing it down**
> lithium cells lose voltage as they age
> the chip needs a voltage floor to hold peak clocks
> when the battery cannot hold it under load
> the phone throttles instead of shutting off
> so your two year old phone is not old
> **it is running on a tired battery**
> eighty dollars for a new cell gets you most of that speed back
> people replace the whole phone for a part that costs eighty dollars

Evergreen, so it works any week. Defuses a conspiracy everyone already
believes, which is a strong shape — you are telling people something they feel
is true and giving them the real reason plus a fix.
