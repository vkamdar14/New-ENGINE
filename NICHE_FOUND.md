# Niche: EA FC 26 tutorial Shorts

## The proof

**"the red3"** — 1,610 subscribers, 76 videos, channel is **50 days old**,
**4.1M total views**. Top video: 1,117,968 views on a 1,610-sub channel.

```
duration      11s median
cadence       10.6 videos/week
views         p25 3,609   p50 7,880   p75 29,649   p90 43,423
hit rate      4 of 69 videos over 100k  (6%)
top 4         1.1M · 534k · 503k · 331k
```

They did not have an audience. The format did the work.

## The competition is essentially absent

One search page of FC 26 tutorial Shorts from the last 45 days:

| 75th percentile | 7,060 subs |

The median channel competing here has three hundred subscribers. That is a
niche nobody has claimed yet.

## Why this is replicable

Requires a copy of EA FC 26, screen recording, and basic caption editing.
No face, no voice, no football ability, no filming. An 11-second video is
maybe 20 minutes of work end to end once the template exists.

Compare the alternatives found in the same harvest: skill/freestyle channels
(*Neymar skill*, *GHUFRAN SKILL MASTER*) need you to actually be good at
football; tactics channels (*On The Pitch IQ*, 35M views) need match footage
you do not own.

## The format, exactly

```
How to [specific mechanic] in FC 26 🔥
```

Their whole catalogue: defending, long passes, GK passes, volleys, corner
kicks, power headers, long range. One mechanic per video. 7-16 seconds.
Occasionally the tier variant: `Noob vs Casual vs Pro`.

Structure inside 11 seconds:
1. Text states the promise on frame one
2. The input shown on screen
3. The result

No intro, no outro, no talking.

## The honest numbers

**Median video: 7,880 views.** The 1.1M is the outlier, not the expectation.
Their model is 10 posts a week where 6% break 100k — the winners pay for the
rest. Do not plan around the top video.

**Gaming RPM is the worst of any niche** — roughly $2 long-form, and Shorts
earn about 2% of that. 4.1M views here is worth tens of dollars in ad revenue,
not thousands. This niche builds an audience fast and monetises badly; the
money comes later, from what you point that audience at.

**The window closes.** FC 26 tutorial demand is tied to this game's cycle.
The reason competition is thin is that the game is current and nobody has
saturated it yet — that is exactly the thing that changes.

## What to do

1. 10 videos a week, one mechanic each, 10-13 seconds.
2. Copy their title template literally: `How to [mechanic] in FC 26 🔥`.
3. Text promise on frame one, sound-off legible.
4. Ship 30 before judging anything. At a 6% hit rate, ten videos tells you
   nothing.
