# Channels where most videos clear 100k — and which are still working

You asked for channels where a lot of videos get over 100k, not lottery
tickets. Here they are, with the check that matters most.

## The trap in the aggregate

A channel's all-time hit rate hides whether the format still works. Splitting
each catalogue in half by date and comparing the recent median to the older
one changes the answer completely:

Six of the twelve are falling. On The Pitch IQ looks like the best small-channel
target in the world on its aggregate — 72% over 100k, 392k median — and its
last ten videos average 148k against an older 781k. Dribble Labs looks like a
51% hit rate and its last ten did 21,806.

**Copy either of those and you would be copying a format that already stopped
working.** Always split by date before believing a hit rate.

## The two that are actually working

### NextGenFootball2 — 59,900 subs, 8.82x rising

The smallest channel still climbing, and it is not close.

```
duration   11s
cadence    9.2/week
recent     2,626,619 · 1,355,639 · 286,575 · 269,523 · 139,682 · 126,999
format     "HOW TO LEARN FLICK UP SKILL TUTORIAL"
```

One skill per video, 8-18 seconds, shot on a phone. **Needs you to actually be
able to do the skill** — that is the entire barrier, and it is why the niche
stays uncrowded at 59k subs while doing half a million a video.

### HD16 — 476,000 subs, 4.92x rising

```
duration   24s
cadence    5.2/week
format     match moment + sentimental caption
recent     "Cancelo Is Only Happy At Barca ❤️‍🩹" 853,403
           "Alvarez Didn't Deserve This 😢" 488,204
           "Messi Brings Joy To Fans ❤️‍🩹" 191,158
```

No filming, no skill — an existing clip plus one emotional line and an emoji.
The barrier here is not talent, it is **footage rights**: every clip is
broadcast material. That is the same permission question as the DrDonut work,
and it is yours to answer before building on it.

## What this means for you

If you can do football skills on camera, NextGenFootball2's format is the
strongest thing in this entire analysis — 11-second videos, phone camera, a
ball, and a niche whose barrier keeps competitors out.

If you cannot, the honest position is that every no-skill football format I
measured is either falling (Dribble Labs, On The Pitch IQ), needs licensed
footage (HD16), needs a game you do not own (FC26 Tutorials), or has a 1,100-
view median floor (quizzes).

That is a real answer, not a hedge: football's consistent-100k formats are
gated behind either ability or footage, and the ungated ones are cooling.
